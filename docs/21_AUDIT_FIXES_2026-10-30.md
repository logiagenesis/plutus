## New: payments
Record payment on any issued invoice (date, amount, EFT/cash/card/other, bank reference). Cannot exceed what is owed; same click twice records once; removing a payment keeps it in history as removed. Invoice status: Paid / Part paid / Unpaid / Overdue for invoices made in Plutus. Imported Sage invoices stay "Not checked" until a payment is recorded, because Sage has no payments after February 2022.
