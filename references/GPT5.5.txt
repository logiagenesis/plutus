Copy# Logi-Ink Accounting System Audit

## Project

Build an in-house accounting system for Logia / Logi-Ink with only the functionality actually needed:

- Quoting
- Invoicing
- Recurring invoices
- Customer statements
- Bank statement imports
- Bank transaction matching
- Basic reports

This system is not intended to replace a full enterprise accounting platform. It is intended to remove the cost and complexity of Sage where the business only needs a focused internal tool.

## Source Systems Reviewed

### Sage Accounting South Africa

Sage Accounting is a cloud-based accounting system that works through a browser and across PC, Mac, smartphone, and tablet devices. Sage states that the product syncs work across devices, automatically backs up data in the cloud, and does not require local installation.  
Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/

### Sage One Accounting Login

Sage One Accounting login confirms the platform entry point and password reset/support workflow for the accounting.sageone.co.za product.  
Source: https://accounting.sageone.co.za/Landing/Default.aspx

### Sage Invoicing

Sage invoicing supports quotes, invoices, recurring invoices, draft invoices, batch invoice sending, invoice branding, PDF invoices, online invoice links, customer payment status, and converting quotes to invoices.  
Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/invoicing/

### Sage Bank Feeds

Sage bank feeds pull bank transactions into Sage Accounting, match transactions to invoices, support split transactions, and learn from user category selections.  
Source: https://www.sage.com/africa/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/connect-my-bank/

### Sage Banking Guide

Sage One banking supports manual transaction capture, OFX imports, CSV imports, automatic bank feeds, mapping rules, split transactions, customer receipt allocation, supplier payment allocation, attachments, reviewed/unreviewed banking states, and batch edits.  
Source: https://accounting.sageone.co.za/resources/pdf/Sage%20One%20Accounting%20Banking%20Guide.pdf

### 6. Bank Transaction Matching

Observed functionality:

Sage automatically matches bank transactions to invoices and uses mapping rules to assign transactions to accounts. Sage One’s banking guide says mapping rules can use keywords in the bank statement description to assign transactions automatically.  
Source: https://accounting.sageone.co.za/resources/pdf/Sage%20One%20Accounting%20Banking%20Guide.pdf

Recommended Logi-Ink matching logic:

Automatic match rules:

- Exact invoice number in bank reference
- Exact invoice amount
- Same customer name in bank description
- Payment date after invoice date
- Payment amount equals outstanding invoice balance

Manual match tools:

- Search invoice
- Match transaction to one invoice
- Split transaction across multiple invoices
- Unmatch transaction
- Mark as unknown
- Mark as non-customer income
- Mark as bank charge
- Mark as owner transfer

Matching confidence:

- `High`
- `Medium`
- `Low`
- `Manual`
