## 3. Sage Accounting SA: features in Logi-Ink scope

### 3.1 Quoting and invoicing
Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/invoicing/

What Sage does:
- Converts a quote to an invoice without re-entering details
- Offers 9 invoice templates (product-based and service-based)
- Lets you customise headings, footer, logo, theme and font colours, prefixes and numbering, contact details, line items (price, rate, qty/hours, discount per line), default T&Cs, and payment notes
- Supports pro-forma and draft invoices
- Tracks when an invoice was sent, viewed and paid
- Has a Pay Now button on the invoice that auto-updates the invoice when the customer pays
- Generates an online version of every invoice (shareable link or PDF)
- Shows the status as outstanding, part paid or paid
- Lets you allocate one payment across multiple invoices
- Has a Quick Entries option for bulk sales invoices and credit notes
- Sends invoices in batches
- Copies invoices and quotes to Google Drive automatically
- Has mobile apps for iOS and Android

### 3.2 Recurring invoices
Sources:
- https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/invoicing/
- https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-accounting/f/general-discussion/205332/can-i-edit-my-description-field-every-month-on-my-recurring-invoices

What Sage does:
- Sends recurring invoices automatically at scheduled intervals, as PDF or online
- Lets you review recurring runs as drafts before sending

What's NOT working (confirmed by Sage's own support reply in the Community thread):
- Editing an item's description in the item list does NOT update existing recurring invoices. Each recurring invoice has to be edited by hand.
- Editing a recurring draft is a one-off fix. It does NOT update the recurring template.
- A user can't add a changing value (e.g. the month) to a line description on each run.

### 3.3 Bank import and reconciliation
Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/connect-my-bank/

What Sage does:
- Has direct bank feeds with FNB, Nedbank and Investec only
- For other banks, the page implies CSV upload ("no more .csv files" is listed as a benefit of the direct feeds)
- Includes automatic bank reconciliation on all plans (per the pricing page)

What's NOT working:
- Direct feeds cover 3 banks only. Banks not listed on Sage's page (e.g. ABSA, Standard Bank, Capitec) have no direct feed.
- The user has to re-confirm bank consent every 3 months.

Source: https://www.xero.com/za/pricing-plans/

| Plan | Regular price (incl. VAT) | Limits in scope |

| Plan | Price (excl. VAT, billed annually) | Users | Invoices/year |

## 6. Build implications for Logi-Ink (from verified gaps only)

| Sage gap (verified) | Logi-Ink requirement |
|---|---|
| Recurring invoices don't pick up item description changes | Recurring templates link to live item data, with an option to lock a line |
| Can't add the month or period to a recurring line | Placeholder tokens in line descriptions, e.g. `{month}` and `{period}`, resolved at each run |
| Editing a draft doesn't update the template | "Apply to template" toggle when editing a recurring draft |
| Direct feeds for 3 banks only | CSV/OFX import with saved column maps per bank (FNB, ABSA, Standard Bank, Nedbank, Capitec, Investec) |
| Add-ons push up the cost | No modules or add-ons; one in-house system |
| Debit order and access lockouts | Self-hosted; our data, no vendor lock |
