Copy# Logi-Ink accounting — scope note

Checked 27 October 2026. Public pages only. Not a full product audit. Sage help, YouTube, and partner "training" sites were not downloaded. I cannot write this into Google Drive or GitHub from here.

## Do not build

Stock, payroll, VAT submission, multi-currency, budgeting, project tracking, time tracking, Debtors Manager, live bank feeds.

Open banking in South Africa needs a registered third party. Do not build a live bank connection in-house.

## What Sage actually costs

Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/pricing/

- Accounting Start: from R270 per month, or from R3,240 per year, incl. VAT. Tier depending.
- Accounting Standard: from R490 per month for 2 users, or from R5,880 per year, incl. VAT. Tier depending.
- Extra user: R85 per month.
- Extra company: R460 per month.
- Advanced Inventory: R465 per month.
- Debtors Manager: R325 per month.
- Time Tracking: R185 per month.
- Multi-currency payments: R185 per month.
- Extra storage, 1 GB: R20 per month.
- 30-day refund if you tell them it is not a fit. No long contract.

Start is the sole-trader plan. Standard adds photo capture of receipts, invoices, and supplier statements. Both plans include the base accounting features. Roles exist, from read-only to full access.

## What Sage does that Logi-Ink actually needs

Source: https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/invoicing/

- Quote converts to invoice without retyping.
- Recurring invoices.
- PDF or online link. Customer can see outstanding, part-paid, paid.
- One receipt can be split across several invoices.
- Line discounts, numbering prefix, logo, terms, notes.
- Quick Entries for batch sales invoices and credit notes.
- Google Drive copy of invoices and quotes.
- Pay Now updates the invoice when the customer pays. South Africa only. Not required for v1.

https://www.sage.com/en-za/sage-business-cloud/accounting/features/connect-my-bank/

## What is broken, from Sage's own forum

Quote status can say Invoiced when no invoice exists. Causes Sage named: user set the status by hand, or converted and did not save. Invoice history shows the source quote. Quote history does not show the invoice number. Sage's reply was to use the audit trail. A user called this a fraud hole. Suggestion logged. No fix shown on the thread.

https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-accounting/f/general-discussion/111765/quotes-converted-to-invoices---history-function

Recurring invoices duplicated 2, 3, and 4 times with the same invoice number, across more than one customer, and reported again months later. Sage workaround: edit the duplicates to zero so they drop off the statement. Not a fix.

https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-accounting/f/general-discussion/200966/duplication-of-recurring-invoices

Same thread, user-reported, not verified by me: outbound email blocked despite SPF and whitelisting, year-to-date reports showing life-to-date, custom views resetting, lockouts, monthly server crashes, bank feed failures, bank import failures, same settings behaving differently across companies on one profile.

## Build this

1. Customers, quotes, invoices. A quote becomes an invoice only when the invoice row is saved. Store the quote id on the invoice and the invoice id on the quote. Do not allow a manual status of Invoiced.
2. Recurring invoices. A schedule creates one draft per period, with its own number. A lock stops the same period running twice. Send mail from logia.co.za, not a shared vendor server.
3. Statements. A date-range PDF of open invoices. Not a separate document type. Path in the product can mirror Sage: customer, date range, send.
4. Bank import. CSV first, three columns, same rule as Sage, plus OFX and QIF. Suggest a match on amount, date window, and reference. A person confirms every match. No live bank login.

Out of v1: stock, payroll, VAT 201 filing, multi-currency, budgets, projects, time tracking, payment links.
